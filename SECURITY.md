# Security policy

Please do not raise security issues in public issues or pull requests.

Report suspected vulnerabilities to the service's security contact (replace with your
team's address, e.g. `security@example.gov.uk`) with steps to reproduce. We acknowledge
within 2 working days and agree a fix timeline based on severity
(critical 2 days, high 7 days, medium 30 days).

How the service is secured: [docs/security.md](docs/security.md).
